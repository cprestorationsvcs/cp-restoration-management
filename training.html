Before You Begin
  • CP Restoration Services has been restoring credit since 2014. We have helped 7,000+ clients improve their credit scores.
  • All client information is strictly confidential. You signed a Non-Compete and Confidentiality Agreement — violating it results in immediate termination and legal action.
  • You are engaged as an independent contractor. You are responsible for your own taxes.
  • All work hours must be logged in the CP Restoration time tracker. Screenshots are taken every 10 minutes. Time theft results in immediate termination.
  • Payments are released every Friday via PayPal or Wise. You must have your payment details on file before your first Friday.
