💬 STEP 3 — What to Say on the Call

"Hi, may I speak with [Client Name]?

Hi [Name], my name is [Your Name] calling from CP Restoration Services. I am reaching out today to give you an update on your file and to make sure we have everything we need to keep your disputes moving forward.

Do you have 2 to 3 minutes?"
💡 If they ask about their score: "Our dispute team is actively working on your file. I will submit a status update to your portal today so you can see the progress."
💡 If they are upset: "I completely understand and I appreciate your patience. Let me flag this as urgent for our team right now and make sure someone follows up with you within 24 hours."
💡 If no answer: Leave a voicemail — "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from CP Restoration Services calling to give you an update on your file. Please call us back at 1-855-327-5847 at your convenience. Thank you."
