Company Policies & Agreements

Please read and acknowledge each policy before continuing.

Client Data Confidentiality

All client information including names, Social Security Numbers, credit report data, financial information, and account details are strictly confidential. You may not share, screenshot, copy, or discuss any client information outside of the CP Restoration Services portal or with anyone not employed by CP Restoration Services. Violation of this policy is grounds for immediate termination and may result in legal action.

Portal & System Security

You are responsible for keeping your portal login credentials secure. Do not share your username or password with anyone. Do not access the portal from public Wi-Fi without a VPN. Report any suspected unauthorized access immediately to Jason Crown at cprestorationsvcs@gmail.com.

Screenshot Monitoring

By accepting employment, you consent to automated screenshot monitoring during your shift hours. Screenshots are taken every 8-12 minutes while you are logged into the dashboard. Screenshots are reviewed by management to verify active work during paid hours. Failure to share your screen when prompted is a policy violation.

Professional Standards

All CP Restoration Services team members are expected to maintain the highest level of professionalism in all client interactions. This includes being respectful, patient, and empathetic at all times. You may not make promises about credit score improvements, deletion timelines, or refunds without authorization.

Communication Standards

All client communication must go through the official CP Restoration Services portal. You may not communicate with clients via personal email, phone, or social media. Any attempt to solicit clients for personal gain is grounds for immediate termination.

Attendance & Punctuality

You are expected to be logged into the portal and ready to work at 9:00 AM EST Monday through Friday. Repeated tardiness or unexplained absences will result in disciplinary action. You must notify your supervisor at least 2 hours before your shift if you will be absent.

Pay Rate & Schedule

Compensation is paid at the rate agreed upon during your hiring discussion, paid on a bi-weekly basis via the payment method on file. Pay is based on a standard 40-hour work week (Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST). Time is tracked automatically via the portal dashboard. Your pay rate and first pay date will be confirmed by management separately.

Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted monthly. Pay increases and advancement opportunities are based on performance metrics including client satisfaction, response time, and attendance. Agents who consistently perform above expectations will be considered for additional responsibilities and compensation.
