📌 Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Answer the Call & Pull Up the Client

Answer: "Thank you for calling CP Restoration Services, this is [your name], how can I help you today?"

While they are speaking, open the CS Dashboard and search by their name, phone, or email. Have their record ready before you respond.

🔗 CS Dashboard: portal-cprestorationsvcs.com/cs-dashboard.html
2

Verify Bureau Logins (ALWAYS do this first)

Before anything else — verify that all 3 bureau logins are working. This tells you where they started and how much work has been done.

⚠ ALWAYS verify logins first — whether the client wants a refund OR a status update. You cannot confirm what work has been done without working logins.

Check in Client Manager → Bureau Logins tab:

  • ✅ All 3 bureaus login successfully → file is accessible → work can continue
  • ❌ Any login fails → client may have changed credentials (violation of agreement)
  • 📱 Experian → requires 4-digit PIN and security question answer → verify these are saved in Bureau Logins tab
🔗 Client Manager: portal-cprestorationsvcs.com/client-manager.html
3A

🔴 If Client Wants a REFUND

Stay calm. Do not argue. Do not make promises. Follow this exactly:

  • Acknowledge their concern with empathy
  • Check CS Dashboard → Disputes tab — note what has been deleted (view only, do not change)
  • Inform them work has been completed on their file
  • Tell them: "I am submitting your refund request to our Compliance Department right now. They will review your file and make a decision within 24 hours."
  • Go to CS Dashboard → Actions tab → Send Refund Request to Compliance → select reason → add notes → submit
  • This emails Queen in Compliance automatically
  • If client changed bureau logins — note this in the request — it affects the refund evaluation
  • If client did a chargeback — click Pause File in the CS Dashboard immediately and notify Compliance
❌ YOU CANNOT approve or deny refunds. Queen in Compliance makes all refund decisions. Compliance will review, update the client portal, and email the client within 24 hours. You follow up by phone after.
3B

🟠 If Client Wants a STATUS UPDATE

Follow this exact process every time:

  • Go to CS Dashboard → click the client → click Bureau Logins tab
  • Confirm all 3 bureau logins are on file and correct — update any that are wrong
  • Tell the client: "I am submitting a status update request to our Dispute Department right now. You will receive a full update within 24 hours by email and in your client portal."
  • Go to CS Dashboard → Actions tab → Request Status Update → select reason → add notes → submit
  • This emails the Dispute Department automatically
  • The Dispute Department logs into each bureau, checks all deletions and pending items, updates the system, and sends the client an email within 24 hours
  • The update also appears in the client's portal automatically
  • You call the client to confirm after 24 hours
✅ You submit the request — the Dispute Department does the work. Your job is to verify logins, submit the request, and follow up with the client after 24 hours.
4

Set Up Client Portal Access

Every client should be on the portal. If they are not already using it, set them up on this call:

  • Give them the portal URL: portal-cprestorationsvcs.com/client-portal.html
  • Their User ID is their Client ID (starts with CPR-)
  • Default password: restore2026
  • Tell them: "You can log in anytime to see your dispute progress, and you can send us messages directly through the portal. We respond within 24 hours Monday through Friday."
💬 Remind them: sending messages through the portal is faster than calling — they get a written record of every response.
5

End the Call Professionally

Close every call with: "Thank you for your patience [Name]. We are committed to getting you the best results possible. Is there anything else I can help you with today?"

✅ Log any notes in the CS Dashboard before the next call.

💬 Handling Portal Messages

Clients who message through the portal instead of calling

1

Check the CS Dashboard Daily

Open the CS Dashboard every morning and after every break. Clients with unread messages show a red dot next to their name.

2

Read and Categorize

Read the message and determine: Is this a refund request, a status update request, a question, or a login issue? Follow the same workflow as a phone call.

3

Reply Within 24 Hours

Use the quick reply buttons for common responses or type a custom reply. Your reply is automatically emailed to the client and appears in their portal.

⚠ All messages must be responded to within 24 hours Monday–Friday. Weekend messages are responded to first thing Monday morning.

⏳ Time Tracking — How It Works

Your time is tracked automatically. You do not need to clock in or out manually.
  • Time tracking starts automatically when you log into the dashboard
  • It pauses automatically after 15 minutes of no activity (no mouse movement, no typing, no clicking)
  • It resumes automatically when you start working again
  • It stops when you click Logout
  • A 🟢 Clocked In badge appears in the top bar — click it to see your hours for the day
  • When you first log in, your browser will ask you to share your screen — you must click Share. This is required for screenshot monitoring
⚠️ Important: Do NOT close the browser or navigate away from the portal during your shift without logging out first. Always use the Logout button at the end of your shift so your hours are recorded correctly.

📷 Screenshot Monitoring

CP Restoration Services uses automated screenshot monitoring to ensure team members are actively working during their shift.

  • When you log in, your browser will ask: "Choose what to share"
  • Select Entire Screen and click Share
  • Screenshots are taken automatically every 8–12 minutes
  • Screenshots are reviewed by management and include your name and timestamp
  • This is a condition of employment — failure to share your screen will be noted

🔒 Invalid Bureau Login — CS Agent Protocol

When the Dispute Department cannot log into a client's credit bureau account, they will notify CS. Here is exactly what you do:

  1. Open the client's file in the CS Dashboard
  2. Go to the Actions tab
  3. Click 🔐 Report Invalid Bureau Login
  4. Check which bureau(s) are failing
  5. Call the client using this script:
"Hi, may I speak with [Client Name]? Hi [Client Name], this is [Your Name] from CP Restoration Services. Our dispute team is actively working on your credit file today and we need to verify your [bureau] login credentials to continue processing your disputes. Can you confirm your username and password? [For Experian: also your PIN and security question answer?] Thank you — our team will resume working on your file right away and you'll receive a status update within 24 hours."
  1. Enter the updated credentials in the text box
  2. Click Update & Notify Dispute Department
  3. Update the Bureau Logins tab on the right panel with the new credentials and click Save
⏱ SLA: You must attempt to reach the client within 2 hours of receiving a login issue. Make 3 attempts over 24 hours. If client cannot be reached after 3 attempts, escalate to Jason at cprestorationsvcs@gmail.com.

💰 Refund Request Protocol

  • Refunds are only considered after the service window — clients sign an agreement confirming this
  • If a client demands a refund, be empathetic but do NOT promise one
  • Use the Actions tab → Send Refund Request to submit it to Queen (Compliance)
  • Queen will review and respond within 24 hours
  • The client will be notified automatically by email once Queen approves or denies
Never tell a client their refund is approved. Only Queen can approve refunds. Your job is to submit the request and set the expectation that compliance will review within 24 hours.

📢 Client Communication Rules

  • Always respond to client messages within 2 hours during your shift
  • Never read back a client's Social Security Number — verify identity another way
  • Never make promises about credit score improvements or specific deletion timelines
  • Never discuss another client's file with anyone
  • All sensitive client information is confidential — do not share outside the portal
  • If a client mentions a lawsuit, attorney, or government agency — immediately escalate to Jason
  • If a client mentions Jacob Prado or any former employee who contacted them — immediately escalate to Jason
