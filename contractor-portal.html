💡 Tax Tips for Contractors
Set aside 25-30% of every payment for taxes. As a self-employed contractor, you pay both the employee and employer portions of Social Security and Medicare (15.3% self-employment tax) plus income tax.
Quarterly estimated taxes are due April 15, June 15, September 15, and January 15. Use IRS Form 1040-ES to calculate and pay.
Track your deductible expenses — home office, internet, phone, equipment, and software used for work may be deductible. Keep all receipts.
Your 1099-NEC from CP Restoration Services will show your total gross pay. Report this on Schedule C of your federal tax return.
