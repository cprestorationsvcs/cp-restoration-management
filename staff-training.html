Module 3

CS Dashboard — Core Workflow

The daily workflow for customer service agents.

CS Agents · Compliance

Starting Your Day

  1. Log in and open the CS Dashboard
  2. Clock in at /time-tracker.html
  3. Check Team Chat for overnight alerts and messages from Jason
  4. Check the Call Queue for urgent callbacks — handle these first
  5. Review client list — clients with a red dot have unread messages
  6. Begin working through your client files

Working a Client File

  1. Click the client's name in the left panel to open their file
  2. Review their Info tab — check status, package, and notes
  3. Read any messages in the Messages tab
  4. Take the appropriate action — post an update, send an email, or submit a request
  5. Log all contact in the Contact Log tab with date, type, and outcome

Posting a Status Update

Status updates are visible to clients in their portal. They must be professional and accurate.

  1. Open the client file → click Status Update tab
  2. Select the appropriate status type
  3. Write clear, professional notes explaining what is happening on the file
  4. Click Post Update

💡 Never leave a client without a status update for more than 2 weeks. Even "Your file is active and disputes are pending" is better than silence.

Using the Status Update Tool

For a more detailed update interface, use the Status Update Tool. In the CS Dashboard, open any client → Actions tab → click Open Status Update Tool. It opens pre-loaded for that client.

Client Communication Standards

How we speak to and treat every client.

All Client-Facing Staff

The Standard

Every client paid $2,499 or more. They are not a ticket number — they are someone who trusted us with their financial future. Communicate with that level of respect at all times.

Phone Call Standards

  • Answer with: "Thank you for calling CP Restoration Services, this is [your name], how can I help you today?"
  • Never put a client on hold for more than 2 minutes without checking back
  • If you don't know the answer, say: "Let me look into that for you and get right back to you" — never guess
  • Always end with: "Is there anything else I can help you with today?"
  • Log every call in the Contact Log with date, duration, and outcome

Written Communication Standards

  • Always use proper grammar and spelling — proofread before sending
  • Never use slang, abbreviations, or informal language in client emails
  • Respond to all client messages within 24 hours, Monday through Friday
  • All emails sent through the portal are automatically logged

Things You Must NEVER Say to a Client

🚫 "I don't know" (say "Let me find out for you")
🚫 "That's not my department"
🚫 "We're backed up / we're short staffed"
🚫 "I can't help you with that"
🚫 Any promise of a specific score increase or guaranteed result
🚫 Any comment about legal issues, chargebacks, or lawsuits
🚫 Anything about other clients — never reference other client situations

Module 5

Call Queue & Callbacks

How to manage the urgent callback queue.

CS Agents

What is the Call Queue?

The Call Queue at /call-queue.html captures urgent callback requests from clients — people who called in and need to be called back. These are high priority. A client in the queue has already been waiting.

Working the Queue

  1. Open the Call Queue
  2. Select your name from the "You are:" dropdown
  3. Review all pending items
  4. Click Assign to Me on a card to claim it
  5. Call the client back using RingCentral (RC)
  6. Add your call notes to the card
  7. Click Mark Called when done

Filters

Use the filters at the top — filter by your name and by date. Use "Today" to see only today's callbacks during your shift.

💡 Rule: No callback should sit in the queue for more than 4 hours during business hours. If you see items older than that, handle them or escalate to your supervisor.

Module 6

Dispute Process

How disputes are filed and tracked. For dispute specialists and CS agents alike.

Dispute Specialists · CS Agents

The 35-Day Cycle

Credit bureaus are required to respond to disputes within 35 business days. Our process is built around this cycle. Disputes are filed, the bureau investigates, and the result is logged back in the client's file.

CS Agent Role in Disputes

  • You do NOT file disputes — that is Marcelo and Annabel's job
  • You request disputes via the CS Dashboard → client file → Actions tab → Request Status Update
  • You communicate dispute results TO the client via status updates and email
  • You never tell a client their dispute was "approved" or "removed" unless you can see it confirmed in the file

Dispute Specialist Role

  1. Check Requests Inbox for new CS requests daily
  2. Log into client's bureau accounts
  3. Review negative items and identify what can be disputed
  4. File disputes with the appropriate bureau(s)
  5. Log the dispute in the client's portal file — bureau, item, date filed
  6. Post a status update to the client portal
  7. Email the client a confirmation

🚫 Never log into a bureau account using a personal device or outside of work hours without authorization. Bureau access is logged and monitored.

Compliance & Legal Rules

The non-negotiable rules that protect you, the client, and the company.

All Staff — Mandatory

Federal Law

Credit repair companies are regulated under the Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA). Violations can result in fines, lawsuits, and criminal liability. Take these rules seriously.

Absolute Rules — Zero Tolerance

🚫 Never post SSNs or Social Security numbers anywhere — not in Team Chat, not in client notes, not in email. This is a federal compliance violation and grounds for immediate termination.

🚫 Never promise a specific credit score increase. You can say "we work to improve your score" — never "we will get you to 700."

🚫 Never contact a client with a CHARGEBACK or LEGAL HOLD on their file. Take their number and escalate to Jason immediately.

🚫 Never discuss one client's situation with another client.

🚫 Never authorize a refund — only Jason can approve refunds. You can acknowledge the request and escalate to Queen.

🚫 Never discuss the Xyla situation, former employees, or internal company issues with any client or partner.
