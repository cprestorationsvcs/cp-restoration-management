CP Restoration Services — Client Service Agreement

By signing below, you ("Client") agree to engage CP Restoration Services ("Company") to perform credit restoration services as outlined in the selected package. The Company will dispute inaccurate, unverifiable, or obsolete negative items on your credit reports with Equifax, Experian, and/or TransUnion on your behalf.

Authorization: You authorize the Company to access your credit bureau accounts using the assigned credentials for the sole purpose of filing disputes and monitoring results.

Timeline: Results vary. The Company commits to filing disputes within the agreed timeline. Credit bureaus have 30-45 days to respond per the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA).

Money-Back Guarantee: If no negative items are removed from your credit reports during the service period, you are entitled to a full refund of amounts paid.

Client Obligations: You agree to provide accurate information, complete the bureau access setup as instructed, and not interfere with the dispute process by contacting bureaus directly while disputes are pending.

Cancellation: Either party may cancel with 7 days written notice. Refunds for work completed are not available unless the money-back guarantee applies.
