CP RESTORATION SERVICES REFUND POLICY

Client Satisfaction Commitment. At CP Restoration Services, we take great pride in the satisfaction of our clients. Our primary goal is to establish direct contact with the credit bureaus and advocate on your behalf.

Refund Eligibility — Paid-in-Full Clients. A refund is applicable only if services are paid in full within thirty (30) days of signing up. The money-back guarantee does not apply to clients who utilize split payments, financing, or payment plans, unless otherwise stated in this agreement. Refunds, if approved, will be prorated based on disputed items that have not been successfully removed.

Standard Full Service: If accounts or inquiries included in the service are not removed within 120 business days, a prorated money-back guarantee applies. If any item disputed by CPR is returned as verified, the timeline is extended to 150 business days.

Express Full Service: If accounts or inquiries included in the service are not removed within 60 business days, a prorated money-back guarantee applies. If any item disputed by CPR is returned as verified, the timeline is extended to 90 business days.

Financing / Payment Plan Clients. Refund eligibility exists only if all scheduled payments are made on time and consistently. A one (1) week grace period is permitted for each scheduled payment. Any payment made after the grace period immediately voids all refund eligibility. Financing clients may be eligible for up to 50% of the prorated refund amount if all conditions are met. Failure to remain current on payments results in automatic forfeiture of any refund rights.

Nonrefundable Administrative Fee. A nonrefundable administrative fee of $200 will be deducted from all approved refunds, regardless of payment method. This covers pre-service costs, account setup, compliance preparation, and administrative processing.

Refund Request Process. Upon receipt of a refund request, CPR will conduct a comprehensive review of the client's file including items disputed, items unresolved, and compliance with payment history and contract terms. A determination will be issued within 2 to 3 weeks following completion of the review.

If Eligible: A prorated refund will be issued and the client's file will be closed.
If Client Declines Refund: CPR will proceed with 6 additional rounds of dispute.
If Ineligible for Refund: CPR will at the client's discretion close the file or continue service.

Clients are not permitted to cancel services in order to "wait out" the refund timeframe. Cancellation before the applicable timeframe has fully elapsed forfeits all refund rights.
