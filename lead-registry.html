📞 Call Script

1 Opening — Build Rapport (first 10 seconds)
2 Confirm They Got the Guide
"Hi [Name], I am calling from CP Restoration Services — you recently downloaded our free credit repair guide. Did you get a chance to look at it yet?"

(If yes: "Great! Did anything in there apply to your situation?")
(If no: "No worries at all — that is actually why I am calling, to walk you through the key points.")
3 Identify the Pain Point
"Can I ask — what's going on with your credit right now? Are you dealing with collections, late payments, or anything that is been holding you back?"

(Listen. Let them talk. The more they share, the easier the close.)
4 Present the Solution
"So what we do at CP Restoration is we have a team of dispute specialists who go directly to Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion and challenge every negative item on your report. We've been doing this since 2014 and we've helped over 4,900 clients improve their scores. Most people start seeing results within 60 days."
5 Present Pricing
"We have two programs. Our Express program is $3,999 and gets you results in 60 business days — that is our fastest. Our Standard program is $2,500 and runs 120 business days. We also offer a financing option where you can get started for as little as $750 down and $292 a month. Which of those sounds like it fits your situation best?"
6 Handle Objections
"I need to think about it" — "I completely understand. Can I ask what specifically you need to think about? Is it the price, the timing, or something else? I want to make sure you have everything you need to make the right decision."
"I cannot afford it" — "That's exactly why we have the financing option — you can get started for $750 today and pay $292 a month. Most people find that the improvement in their credit score saves them way more than that in lower interest rates alone."
"I tried credit repair before and it did not work" — "I hear that a lot. The difference with us is we have a dedicated team of dispute specialists, not just automated letters. We work each file manually and we've been doing it since 2014. Can I ask who you used before?"
"Is this legit?" — "Absolutely. We're a Florida-based company, we've been in business since 2014, and all our work is done under the Fair Credit Reporting Act. You can look us up at 35daycreditrepair.com."
7 Close
"Alright [Name], let us get you started. I just need a few minutes to take down your information and we can have your file opened today. Which package works best for you — the Express at $3,999, the Standard at $2,500, or the financing option at $750 down?"
