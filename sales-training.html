💬 The NEPQ Sales Process — How We Close

NEPQ (Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questioning) means asking questions instead of pitching. You are not selling — you are helping the client discover they need the service. The client sells themselves.

Step 1 — Connect (First 30 Seconds)

"Hi, is this [Name]? Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] calling from CP Restoration Services. Do you have about 2 minutes? I want to make sure I am speaking with the right person."

Step 2 — Situation Questions (Understand Their Problem)

"What is your current credit score, do you know off the top of your head?"

"How long has your credit been in this situation?"

"What negative items are showing up — collections, late payments, anything like that?"

"Have you tried to dispute anything on your own before?"

Step 3 — Problem Questions (Make Them Feel the Pain)

"How has having a lower credit score been affecting you — has it impacted your ability to get a loan, rent an apartment, anything like that?"

"What would it mean for you if you could get those negative items removed and raise your score by 50 to 100 points?"

"If your credit was where it needed to be, what would you do first?"

Step 4 — The Bridge (Connect Their Pain to Your Solution)

"Based on everything you have shared with me, it sounds like the negative items on your report are the main thing holding you back. Is that accurate?"

"What we do at CP Restoration is we go in and dispute every one of those negative items directly with the bureaus on your behalf. Our clients typically see their first results within 30 to 60 days. Would that be something that would help your situation?"

Step 5 — Handle Objections

Objection: I do not have the money right now

"I completely understand. Can I ask — if your credit was fixed and you could qualify for a car loan, a mortgage, or even a better apartment, how much would that be worth to you over the next few years? Sometimes the cost of NOT fixing your credit is much higher than the investment to fix it."

Objection: I tried credit repair before and it did not work

"I hear that a lot and I appreciate you being honest with me. Can I ask — what happened? Did the company actually file disputes with the bureaus, or did they just promise results? The reason I ask is that credit repair is a partnership — the company has to do the work AND the client has to stay current going forward. Which part broke down?"

Objection: I need to think about it

"That is totally fair. What specifically do you need to think about? Is it the investment, or is it whether this will actually work for your situation? I want to make sure I answer any questions you have before we hang up."

Step 6 — The Close

"Based on everything we talked about, it sounds like getting these negative items removed would make a real difference for you. Here is how we get started — the investment is $2,000 and we can accept CashApp at $ccpro2020 or Zelle at cprsvcs2026@gmail.com. Once we receive payment we pull your full credit report and start filing disputes within 48 hours. Does that work for you?"
⚠ NEVER promise a specific score increase or guarantee a specific item will be removed. Say "we dispute on your behalf and bureaus are required to investigate" — never guarantee an outcome.

🔒 Compliance — What You Cannot Do

  • ❌ NEVER promise that a specific item will be removed
  • ❌ NEVER collect payment into your personal account — all payments go to CashApp $ccpro2020 or Zelle cprsvcs2026@gmail.com
