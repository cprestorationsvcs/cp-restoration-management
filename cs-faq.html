🚫 Denial & Dispute Situations
❓ Client says: "I don't have an account with you / I never signed up" ▼

Stay calm. Look up the client by name, email and phone in the portal before responding.

"I completely understand your concern. Let me pull up our records right now. Can you give me the email address or phone number you may have used when you signed up? [pause] I am showing a file here under [name] with the email [email]. Does that look familiar to you?

We have been providing credit repair services since 2014 and we do have your file on record. I want to make sure we get this sorted out for you. Can I walk you through what has been done on your file so far?"
⚠ NEVER tell a client they don't have an account until you have searched thoroughly. Try name, email, AND phone number.
❓ Client says: "You are a scam / You are fraud" ▼

Do not argue or get defensive. Acknowledge, de-escalate, and provide proof.

"I am so sorry to hear you feel that way, and I completely understand your frustration. Please let me address this directly.

CP Restoration Services has been in business since 2014 and we have helped over 4,900 clients improve their credit. We are fully licensed and compliant with the Credit Repair Organizations Act.

I would love to walk you through exactly what has been done on your file so you can see the work firsthand. Would you like me to do that right now? I can also send you a written status update by email if you prefer."
💡 If they escalate further, say: "I want to make sure this is handled properly. Let me connect you with our compliance team." Then message Queen in Team Chat immediately.
❓ Client says: "Nothing has been done on my file" ▼

Pull up their file in the portal FIRST before responding. Check the disputes tab and status log.

"I hear you and I want to get to the bottom of this with you right now. Let me pull up your file. [pause]

I can see here that [describe what IS in the file — any disputes filed, dates, bureaus]. Let me walk you through what has been done so far.

[If nothing is there]: I can see your file is active and I am escalating this to our dispute team right now to make sure work begins on your file today. You will receive a written status update within 24 hours. I apologize for the delay."
⚠ If nothing has been done — DO NOT make excuses. Apologize, escalate to dispute team immediately, and follow up to confirm action was taken.
❓ Client says: "I want a refund" ▼

You cannot approve or deny refunds. Your job is to de-escalate and escalate to Jason or Queen.

"I completely understand. I want to make sure your concern is handled at the highest level. Let me escalate this to our owner directly. You will receive a response within 24 business hours.

In the meantime, can I walk you through the work that has been done on your file so far? I want to make sure you have the full picture before a decision is made."
⚠ NEVER say yes or no to a refund. NEVER quote a refund amount. Only Jason or Queen can approve refunds. Tag them in Team Chat immediately with the client name and ID.
❓ Client says: "Someone named [Dean / former agent] contacted me and said..." ▼
"Thank you for letting me know — this is very important. That individual is a former employee who is no longer affiliated with CP Restoration Services and has no authority to represent us, make promises, or collect payments on our behalf.

Please do not send any money or share any personal information with them. If they contact you again, please screenshot the messages and send them to us at cprestorationsvcs@gmail.com or call us at 1-855-327-5847.

Our only official payment channels are CashApp at $ccpro2020 and Zelle at cprsvcs2026@gmail.com. No employee will ever ask you to send money to a personal account."
⚠ Report this to Jason immediately via Team Chat. Note the former employee's name and what they told the client.
❓ Client says: "My score hasn't gone up / Credit repair doesn't work" ▼
"I understand your frustration — you signed up because you wanted results and I respect that.

Credit repair is a process that takes time. Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, bureaus have 30 days to respond to each dispute. Negative items that have been successfully disputed can take another billing cycle or two to reflect on your score.

Let me pull up your file and show you exactly what has been filed so far and what to expect next. [review file] Based on what I see here, [describe disputes filed and timeline]. You should start seeing movement within [realistic timeframe]."
