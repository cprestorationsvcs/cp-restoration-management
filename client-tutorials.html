Before You Begin — 3 Things to Know
  • This takes time and that is normal. Credit restoration is a legal process. The bureaus have 30 days to respond to each dispute. Real, lasting results take months — not days. Clients who stay patient and follow our guidance get the best outcomes.
  • Your actions matter. There are things you must do and things you must avoid while we are working on your file. Module 4 covers this in detail. Following the rules protects your results.
  • We keep you informed. You will receive SMS and email updates every time an action is taken on your file. You can also reach our customer service team anytime during business hours.
