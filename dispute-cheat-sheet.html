📝 How to Post a Status Update

1
Open the client file in CS Dashboard or Client ManagerSearch by name, phone, or client ID
2
Click the Status Log tabThis is where all updates are posted
3
Click Add Status UpdateSelect update type: Dispute, Bureau, Deletion, General
4
Write the updateBe specific — bureau name, items disputed, dates, results
5
Save — client sees it immediately in their portalThe client can view all updates when they log in at client-portal.html
💬 Status update example: "Dispute filed with Experian on 10/09/2026 for 3 items: Capital One collection ($1,200), Late payment — Chase (Mar 2024), Hard inquiry — AMEX (Jan 2024). Bureau has 30 days to respond under FCRA."
